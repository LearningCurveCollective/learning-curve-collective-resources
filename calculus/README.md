# Calculus Resources

This section contains calculus materials emphasizing conceptual understanding of change, accumulation, and approximation.

Topics may include:

- limits and continuity  
- the definition of the derivative and differentiability  
- derivative rules (power, product, quotient, chain) and rates of change  
- related rates, extrema, and derivative tests  
- tangent planes and linear approximation  
- optimization  
- integrals and accumulation  
- multivariable reasoning and geometric intuition
