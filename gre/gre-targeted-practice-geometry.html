Formulas for geometry essentials

Right triangles

$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$

$c$ is the hypotenuse: the side across from the right angle, and the longest side.

Common side sets: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25, and multiples such as 6-8-10.

Special right triangles

In a 30-60-90 triangle, $x$ is across from $30^\circ$, $x\sqrt{3}$ is across from $60^\circ$, and $2x$ is the hypotenuse.

Areas

Triangle: $\dfrac{1}{2}bh$

Rectangle: $lw$

Parallelogram: $bh$

Trapezoid: $\dfrac{1}{2}(b_1 + b_2)h$

The height is perpendicular to the base. In a parallelogram it is not the slanted side.

Tangent lines

A tangent line meets the radius at a right angle at the point where it touches the circle.

$OT^2 + PT^2 = OP^2$

Circles

Circumference: $2\pi r$.   Area: $\pi r^2$.

Arc length: $\dfrac{\text{angle}}{360} \times 2\pi r$.   Sector area: $\dfrac{\text{angle}}{360} \times \pi r^2$.

Coordinate plane

$\text{distance}^2 = (\text{change in } x)^2 + (\text{change in } y)^2$

This is the Pythagorean theorem with the two changes as legs.

Slope $= \dfrac{\text{change in } y}{\text{change in } x}$. Perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is $-1$.

Circles in the coordinate plane

$(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2$

The center is $(h, k)$ and the radius is $r$.

For a point, find $(\text{change in } x)^2 + (\text{change in } y)^2$ from the center and compare it with $r^2$: less means inside, equal means on the circle, greater means outside.

Solids and water level

Rectangular solid: $V = lwh$.   Cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$.

A solid placed under water raises the level by (volume of the solid) $\div$ (area of the tank's base).

What to check before you move on

  • Which side is across from the right angle? That side is the hypotenuse.
  • Is my height perpendicular to the base?
  • Am I comparing with $r$ or with $r^2$?
  • Should the answer be a length, an area, or a volume?

The angles of a triangle measure $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, and $90^\circ$, and the hypotenuse has length 12. What is the area of the triangle?

Answer C ($18\sqrt{3}$)

Givens Angles $30^\circ$, $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$;  Hypotenuse 12

Plan The hypotenuse is $2x$. Find $x$, then the two legs, then use the legs as base and height.

Steps

  1. $2x = 12$, so $x = 6$. That is the leg across from $30^\circ$.
  2. The leg across from $60^\circ$ is $x\sqrt{3} = 6\sqrt{3}$.
  3. Area $= \dfrac{1}{2}(6)(6\sqrt{3}) = 18\sqrt{3}$.
Check. $6^2 + (6\sqrt{3})^2 = 36 + 108 = 144 = 12^2$, and $18\sqrt{3} \approx 31.1769$. ✓
Common mistake. Using the hypotenuse as the base with a leg as the height, which gives 36 or $36\sqrt{3}$. The two legs are the sides that meet at the right angle, so they are the base and the height.

A parallelogram has sides of length 9 and 4, and one of its interior angles measures $150^\circ$. What is the area of the parallelogram?

Enter your answer as an integer.

Answer 18

Givens Sides 9 and 4;  One interior angle is $150^\circ$

Plan Neighboring angles of a parallelogram add to $180^\circ$, so the other angle is $30^\circ$. Use the side of length 9 as the base and find the perpendicular height.

Steps

  1. The acute angle is $180 - 150 = 30^\circ$.
  2. Drop a perpendicular to the side of length 9. The side of length 4 is the hypotenuse of a 30-60-90 triangle, so the height is $\dfrac{4}{2} = 2$.
  3. Area $= 9 \times 2 = 18$.
Check. With the side of length 4 as the base, the height is $\dfrac{9}{2} = 4.5$, and $4 \times 4.5 = 18$. ✓
Common mistake. Multiplying the two sides, $9 \times 4 = 36$. The slanted side is not the height unless the angle between the sides is $90^\circ$.

Point $P$ lies outside a circle with center $O$ and radius 5. A line through $P$ is tangent to the circle at point $T$, and $PT = 12$. What is the distance from $P$ to $O$?

Enter your answer as an integer.

Answer 13

Givens Radius $OT = 5$;  Tangent segment $PT = 12$

Plan The radius and the tangent meet at a right angle at $T$. So $OP$ is across from the right angle, which makes it the hypotenuse.

Steps

  1. $OP^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 = 25 + 144 = 169$.
  2. $OP = 13$.
Check. 5-12-13 is a common side set for right triangles. ✓
Common mistake. Treating $OP$ as a leg and computing $\sqrt{12^2 - 5^2} \approx 10.9$. Find the right angle first: it is at $T$, so the side that does not touch $T$ is the hypotenuse.

Numeric Entry

In the $xy$-plane, a circle has center $(2, -1)$ and passes through the point $(8, 7)$. What is the radius of the circle?

Enter your answer as an integer.

Answer 10

Givens Center $(2, -1)$;  The circle passes through $(8, 7)$

Plan The radius is the distance from the center to any point on the circle.

Steps

  1. Change in $x$: $8 - 2 = 6$. Change in $y$: $7 - (-1) = 8$.
  2. $r^2 = 6^2 + 8^2 = 100$.
  3. $r = 10$.
Check. 6-8-10 is a multiple of 3-4-5. ✓
Common mistake. Computing the change in $y$ as $7 - 1 = 6$. Subtracting $-1$ adds 1, so the change is 8. Entering 100, which is $r^2$, is a second slip to watch for.

A circle has radius 9. What is the length of an arc of the circle that has a central angle of $80^\circ$?

Answer B ($4\pi$)

Givens Radius 9;  Central angle $80^\circ$

Plan An arc is the same fraction of the circumference as its angle is of $360^\circ$.

Steps

  1. $\dfrac{80}{360} = \dfrac{2}{9}$.
  2. Circumference: $2\pi(9) = 18\pi$.
  3. Arc: $\dfrac{2}{9} \times 18\pi = 4\pi$.
Check. $4\pi \approx 12.5664$, and the whole circumference is about 56.5487. The ratio is about 0.2222, which is $\dfrac{2}{9}$. ✓
Common mistake. Choosing $18\pi$. That is the area of the sector, $\dfrac{2}{9} \times 81\pi$, and it also happens to equal the circumference. The question asks for a length along the circle.

A rectangular tank has a base that measures 25 centimeters by 16 centimeters. When a stone is placed in the tank and is completely under water, the water level rises by 0.5 centimeter, and no water overflows. What is the volume of the stone, in cubic centimeters?

Enter your answer as an integer.

Answer 200

Givens Base: 25 cm by 16 cm;  Rise in water level: 0.5 cm

Plan The stone pushes aside a volume of water equal to its own volume. That water forms a layer over the whole base.

Steps

  1. Area of the base: $25 \times 16 = 400$ square centimeters.
  2. Volume: $400 \times 0.5 = 200$ cubic centimeters.
Check. $\dfrac{200}{400} = 0.5$ centimeter of rise. ✓
Common mistake. Dividing 400 by 0.5 to get 800. Volume is base area times height, and here the height is the 0.5 centimeter rise.

Quantitative Comparison
In the $xy$-plane, a circle has center $(2, 2)$ and radius 5. Point $P$ has coordinates $(-1, 6)$.
Quantity A
The distance from $P$ to the center of the circle
Quantity B
$5$

Answer C (The two quantities are equal)

Givens Center $(2, 2)$; radius 5;  $P = (-1, 6)$;  Quantity A: the distance from $P$ to the center; Quantity B: $5$

Plan Use the distance formula from the center to $P$.

Steps

  1. Change in $x$: $-1 - 2 = -3$. Change in $y$: $6 - 2 = 4$.
  2. $\text{distance}^2 = (-3)^2 + 4^2 = 25$.
  3. Distance $= 5$. The quantities are equal, so $P$ is on the circle.
Check. 3-4-5 is a common side set for right triangles. ✓
Common mistake. Comparing 25 with the radius 5 and choosing A. Compare the distance with $r$, or the squared distance with $r^2$, but keep both sides in the same form.

Point $P$ lies outside a circle with center $O$ and radius 7. A line through $P$ is tangent to the circle at point $T$, and $PT = 24$. What is the distance from $P$ to the point on the circle that is closest to $P$?

Answer B (18)

Givens Radius $OT = 7$;  Tangent segment $PT = 24$

Plan Find $OP$ from the right triangle $OTP$. The closest point on the circle lies on segment $OP$, one radius away from $O$.

Steps

  1. $OP^2 = 7^2 + 24^2 = 49 + 576 = 625$, so $OP = 25$.
  2. Closest point: $25 - 7 = 18$ from $P$.
Check. 7-24-25 is a common side set for right triangles. The closest point is nearer than $T$, and $18 < 24$. ✓
Common mistake. Answering 24, the length of the tangent segment, or 25, the distance to the center. The question asks for the distance to the circle itself.
9
Quantitative Comparison
The hypotenuse of an isosceles right triangle has length 8.
Quantity A
The length of one leg of the triangle
Quantity B
$5.5$

Answer A (Quantity A is greater)

Givens Isosceles right triangle; hypotenuse 8;  Quantity A: one leg; Quantity B: $5.5$

Plan In a 45-45-90 triangle the hypotenuse is $x\sqrt{2}$, where $x$ is a leg.

Steps

  1. $x\sqrt{2} = 8$, so $x = \dfrac{8}{\sqrt{2}} = 4\sqrt{2}$.
  2. $4\sqrt{2} \approx 5.6569$, and $5.6569 > 5.5$. Quantity A is greater.
Check. $5.6569^2 + 5.6569^2 \approx 64.0010$, which is $8^2$ up to rounding. ✓
Common mistake. Dividing the hypotenuse by 2 to get a leg of 4, and choosing B. The hypotenuse is $\sqrt{2}$ times a leg, and $\sqrt{2}$ is about 1.4142, not 2.

Numeric Entry

In the $xy$-plane, a parallelogram has vertices $(0, 0)$, $(6, 0)$, $(8, 4)$, and $(2, 4)$. What is the area of the parallelogram?

Enter your answer as an integer.

Answer 24

Givens Vertices $(0, 0)$, $(6, 0)$, $(8, 4)$, $(2, 4)$

Plan Two sides are horizontal, so use one of them as the base. The height is the vertical distance between those two sides.

Steps

  1. Base: from $(0, 0)$ to $(6, 0)$, length 6.
  2. Height: from $y = 0$ to $y = 4$, which is 4.
  3. Area $= 6 \times 4 = 24$.
Check. The surrounding rectangle is 8 by 4, with area 32. Two right triangles with legs 2 and 4 are cut off, each with area 4. $32 - 8 = 24$. ✓
Common mistake. Multiplying the base by the slanted side, $6 \times \sqrt{20} \approx 26.83$. The slanted side has length $\sqrt{2^2 + 4^2}$, and it is not the height.

In the $xy$-plane, a circle has center $(3, -2)$ and radius 13. Which of the following points lie on the circle?

Indicate all such points.

Answer A, C, D, E

Givens Center $(3, -2)$; radius 13, so $r^2 = 169$

Plan For each point, add the squares of the changes in $x$ and $y$ from the center, and compare the total with 169.

Steps

  1. A $(8, 10)$: $5^2 + 12^2 = 169$. On the circle.
  2. B $(10, 9)$: $7^2 + 11^2 = 170$. Not on the circle.
  3. C $(3, 11)$: $0^2 + 13^2 = 169$. On the circle.
  4. D $(-9, 3)$: $(-12)^2 + 5^2 = 169$. On the circle.
  5. E $(16, -2)$: $13^2 + 0^2 = 169$. On the circle.
  6. F $(12, 7)$: $9^2 + 9^2 = 162$. Not on the circle.
Check. A and D use the 5-12-13 side set, and C and E lie straight above and straight to the right of the center, 13 units away. ✓
Common mistake. Selecting B because 170 is close to 169, or comparing the totals with 13 instead of with 169. A point is on the circle only when the total equals $r^2$.

In triangle $ABC$, angle $C$ measures $90^\circ$, angle $A$ measures $30^\circ$, and $BC = 5$. What is the perimeter of triangle $ABC$?

Answer D ($15 + 5\sqrt{3}$)

Givens Angle $C = 90^\circ$; angle $A = 30^\circ$, so angle $B = 60^\circ$;  $BC = 5$

Plan Match each side with the angle across from it. Side $BC$ is across from angle $A$.

Steps

  1. $BC$ is across from the $30^\circ$ angle, so $x = 5$.
  2. $AB$ is across from the right angle: $2x = 10$.
  3. $AC$ is across from the $60^\circ$ angle: $x\sqrt{3} = 5\sqrt{3}$.
  4. Perimeter: $5 + 10 + 5\sqrt{3} = 15 + 5\sqrt{3}$.
Check. $5^2 + (5\sqrt{3})^2 = 25 + 75 = 100 = 10^2$, and the perimeter is about 23.6603. ✓
Common mistake. Treating $BC$ as the hypotenuse because it is the only length given, which leads to $7.5 + 2.5\sqrt{3}$. A side is named by its two endpoints and lies across from the third vertex.
