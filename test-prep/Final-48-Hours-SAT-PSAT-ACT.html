Test morningNothing rushed, nothing new

You prepared. The only job now is to run the plan.

Go

In the roomThe playbook on one screen

  1. 1
    First minute of every modulesection: say C L E A R, breathe once, go. On paper, write your halfway checkpoint at the top first.
  2. 2
    Read the question before the passage. Every question, every time.
  3. 3
    One clock check at the halfway question. Adjust once, not constantly.
  4. 4
    Thirty seconds without a plan: placeholder answer, mark itflag or circle it, move on.
  5. 5
    Cross out wrong choices with the ABC eliminatoron screen or in the booklet, not in your head.
  6. 6
    Answer everything. No penalty for wrong answers; a blank is the only sure zero.
  7. 7
    Last 90 seconds: fill every blank with your letter of the day, then return to one marked question.
  8. 8
    The break: stand up, eat, drink water. Do not relive the section. It is over and it went fine.

One SAT-specific thing: Module 1 sets your ceiling, so bring your sharpest focus to it. Type fractions as fractions (2/3); decimals need three digits (.667, never .67).
