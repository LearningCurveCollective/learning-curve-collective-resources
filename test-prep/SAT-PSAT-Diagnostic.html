Directions

The questions in this section test reading and writing skills. Each question comes with a short passage, and some passages include a table or graph. Read the passage and the question, then choose the one best answer from the four choices.

Tools on this screen, exactly as in Bluebook:

  • Annotate (highlights and notes): drag across words in the passage or question and a small toolbar appears. Tap a highlight color (yellow, pink, or blue) or underline and it is applied right away. Tap the pencil instead if you want to attach a short note. Click any highlight later to see the note, change it, or delete it. Highlights stay on that question only. A tip from us: highlights are cheap, notes are expensive. A two-word note like A or C on a question you marked for review is worth it; a sentence is not.
  • Mark for Review (the bookmark beside the question number): flags a question so you can find it again on the review page.
  • ABC (top right of the question): the answer eliminator. Turn it on and a strike button appears beside each choice. Cross out the ones you have ruled out; Undo brings one back.
  • Question X of Y (bottom center): opens the review page, which shows every question in this section, which ones are answered, and which ones you marked. Click any number to jump to it.
  • Hide next to the timer hides the clock. It comes back on its own with five minutes left.

When you press Next on the last question you will see the review page. Press Next there to move on to Math. Once you move on, you cannot come back to this section.

The questions in this section test math skills. Some are multiple-choice and some are write-in (grid-in) questions where you type the answer yourself. You may use the built-in calculator (the Calculator button at the top), your own calculator, and scratch paper. The Reference button opens the formula sheet.

Write-in (grid-in) math questions: how to enter answers
  • Enter only one answer, even if you find more than one.
  • Max 5 characters, or 6 if it starts with a negative sign.
  • Fractions are fine if they fit (e.g. 7/2). If a fraction doesn't fit, enter its decimal instead.
  • If a decimal is too long, fill all 5 characters by truncating or rounding the last digit: 2/3 becomes .6666, .6667, 0.666, or 0.667, but never .66 or 0.67.
  • Mixed numbers: enter 3½ as 7/2 or 3.5 (never 31/2).
  • No symbols: %, $, commas, or spaces.
