Start here

This sheet is practice only: it has no new moves. It gathers questions for the 17 moves taught in SAT Desmos 1: Core Moves and SAT Desmos 2: Next Steps, so you can practice choosing a move as well as using one. As you practice, start by identifying the question type. Then decide whether Desmos or algebra makes more sense, and check that your answer addresses what the question asks.

Skill Practice: quick drills

There is no time limit here. These quick questions are about choosing an approach, entering it, and reading what Desmos reports. Open the calculator when you want to try an entry. You can enter fractions as 7/3 or 2.333; both are accepted. Timer 0:00
4

Timed Practice Set

Fifteen SAT-style questions in the two digital-test formats: four-choice multiple choice and student-produced response (type the number). They move from easier questions to harder ones, and they are mixed: no label names the move, so start by identifying the question type. Then decide whether Desmos or algebra makes more sense; on several of these, algebra may be quicker. Turn on the timer if you would like to practice pacing. The calculator starts blank, as it will on test day, so type each line yourself. After you finish and score the set, each solution shows a Desmos approach and a second approach, usually algebra, with a Load into Desmos button so you can compare the lines with your own.

3

Practice by move

37 questions, grouped by the move they practice. Choose By move to work on one skill at a time: each group is headed by its move and the sheet that teaches it. Choose Shuffled to mix the questions and practice identifying the question type; the move label appears after you check each answer. Work each question in the calculator or on paper, then check it right away. Each solution shows the lines to type, with a Load into Desmos button, and ends with the move to remember and a link to its lesson. A ★ starred move chip marks an essential move; extra marks a move that comes up less often.
