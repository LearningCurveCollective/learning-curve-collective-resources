SAT Math: Desmos Strategies

This guide is now the SAT Desmos series

SAT Math: Desmos Strategies has been replaced by three sheets. Its techniques, practice questions and timed set have moved into them.

Start here SAT Desmos 1: Core Moves → Nine skills for working from a graph: intercepts, intersections, counting solutions, sliders, inequalities, statistics, and a line of best fit. The answer-entry practice and the formula list are on its Bluebook Tools tab. Then SAT Desmos 2: Next Steps → Eight further skills built around the tilde: finding unknown constants, reading the report, systems in brackets, equivalent expressions, and the touching condition. Practice SAT Desmos 3: Mixed Practice → Practice only: the practice questions and the 15-question timed set from this guide, plus new questions, grouped by skill or shuffled.

Answers saved on the earlier guide do not carry over to the new sheets.
